## Authoring contract

The original prompts below describe the first five-layer pilot. The approved seven-part extension reuses the same master PNGs byte-for-byte and adds `registered-assembly-v2.json`; v1 geometry remains frozen for archived recipes. In v2, central pelvis and front armor form the hips module. Each leg owns its hip connector and outer hip armor, avoiding exposed missing regions when swapping differently shaped hips. Legs mount to the selected hips, which mount to the selected torso. All seven layers use uniform similarity transforms, with no independent regeneration or axis stretching.

- Prompts express artistic intent, not pixel-accurate guarantees. Actual per-model connection points, helmet crown, shoulder-to-wrist and waist-to-sole segments are calibrated in `src/adapters/assets/registered-assembly.json`.
- Each selected module is a polygon-masked view of its model's actual assembly master. Runtime similarities preserve aspect ratio and align semantic segments. No whole-model image replaces a mixed recipe, and no individual part is independently regenerated or re-enlarged.
- Body-relative joints determine other parts' mounts. Without a torso, the reference-frame joints keep partial modules in their proper slot positions. Layer order is legs, arms, torso, head.
- Small colored extraction fringes remain at a few silhouette pixels in the source artwork. At normal UI size they are minor; poster-quality high-resolution artwork may need further manual/AI cleanup with registration revalidation. The pilot does not claim exact manufacture-grade or official proportions.
