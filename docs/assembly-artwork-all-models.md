## Module registration

`src/adapters/assets/registered-assembly-v3.json` contains all seven body masks, actual source dimensions and semantic joints per model. Leg sockets belong to selected hips, and hips attach to selected torso. Front pelvis armor belongs to hips; hip connectors and side guards belong to each leg. MECHA-03 cannon and compact MECHA-04/06 backpacks travel with torso. Wing Zero EW's existing wings remain a separate collectible (stage eight) outside the seven body-mixing slots; the body master omits them. Previous v1/v2 profile files remain unchanged.

Masked layers must exclude neighboring modules and head antennas from backpack/torso cutouts. Some fine colored extraction fringes remain in generated alpha silhouettes; high-resolution promotional art may need further cleanup and registration verification. Generation does not guarantee exact joints; actual calibrated geometry is authoritative.
